### 5. Measurement Guidelines

#### Minimum Measurement Duration

| Measurement Type | Minimum Duration | Recommended |
|------------------|------------------|-------------|
| Annual average | 90 days (heating season) | 1 year |
| Screening | 7 days | 14 days |
| Post-remediation | 90 days | 1 year |

**Note:** Vacancy periods should not exceed 20% of total measurement duration.

#### Recommended Measurement Locations

**Single-Family Homes:**
- At least 2 separate occupied rooms (living room, bedroom, children's room)
- Preferably on lowest occupied floor or rooms with ground contact
- Additional: basement or high-potential area (e.g., cellar with earth floor)

**Multi-Family Buildings:**
- Focus on lower floor units
- 2 rooms per dwelling unit
- Representative sample of building

**Schools / Kindergartens:**
- All regularly occupied classrooms
- Basement rooms used for activities
- Staff rooms with regular occupancy

#### Sensor Placement

- Place at breathing height (~1-1.5m above floor)
- Minimum 1 meter from windows, doors, and exterior walls
- Avoid direct sunlight
- Away from heat sources (radiators, electronics, fireplaces)
- Not in areas with high humidity (kitchen, bathroom)
- Do not move sensor during measurement period

## Reference Values Summary

### Indoor Air Quality Standards

| Parameter | Excellent | Acceptable | Elevated | Action Required |
|-----------|-----------|------------|----------|-----------------|
| **Radon** | < 100 Bq/m³ | < 300 Bq/m³ | 300-1000 Bq/m³ | > 1000 Bq/m³ |
| **CO2** | < 800 ppm | < 1000 ppm | 1000-1500 ppm | > 1500 ppm |
| **PM2.5** | < 10 µg/m³ | < 15 µg/m³ | 15-35 µg/m³ | > 35 µg/m³ |
| **PM10** | < 20 µg/m³ | < 45 µg/m³ | 45-100 µg/m³ | > 100 µg/m³ |
| **Temperature** | 20-22°C | 18-24°C | 16-18°C or 24-26°C | < 16°C or > 26°C |
| **Humidity** | 40-60% | 30-70% | 20-30% or 70-80% | < 20% or > 80% |

### Applicable Standards

- **Switzerland:** Strahlenschutzverordnung (StSV), SIA 382/1
- **EU:** EN 13779, EU Directive 2013/59/EURATOM
- **WHO:** Air Quality Guidelines (2021)
- **ASHRAE:** Standard 62.1 (Ventilation)
